# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is a small project, and the bar is simple: treat other people well. Everyone taking
part in this repository, whether opening an issue, reviewing a change, or replying in a
discussion, is expected to help keep it a place people are glad to contribute to.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. A "no" to a feature is usually about
  keeping the project small, not about the quality of your idea.
- Be patient. This is maintained in spare time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including anything targeting someone's background,
  identity, or beliefs.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or repeatedly reopening settled decisions.

## Scope

This applies in every space connected to the project: issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and code comments.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a private report through the repository's security advisory
form, or by contacting the maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Reports are
handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that do not
follow this document. Serious or repeated breaches result in a permanent ban from the
project.
